## Design Context

### Users
Small-operator / hustler / affiliate creators trying to break into faceless short-form content (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts). They have time scarcity and budget sensitivity. They're shopping multiple tools at once and dropping anything that feels like vapor or babysits them. They want to set things up once and walk away.

The job to be done: offload one of three workflows — generating AI faceless videos from a niche, repurposing long-form into shorts, or scheduling content they already make.

### Brand Personality
Professional, confident, practical. Statements over slogans. Every claim backed by shipped code.

Three-word voice: **direct, dogfounded, dependable.** (Founder is in the same boat as the customer, but the surface speaks like a product, not a confessional.)

Reference brands for tone: **Linear, Vercel, Cal.com.** Clean, product-first, outcome-oriented.

The interface should evoke: confidence the system will run unattended, calm trust that posts will actually ship.

### Aesthetic Direction
Mature SaaS. The dragon brand identity survives as a single monochrome mark; the rest of the surface is neutral with one accent. Anti-references: corporate buzzwords, "industry-leading", "revolutionize", neon glow, glassmorphism, fire/lava palettes.

- **Theme:** dark default. Most users open this product at night, in a dim office, after their day job. Light mode supported via toggle (don't ship dark-only) but dark is the canonical surface.
- **Palette:** single blue accent (#3b82f6) on neutral surfaces tinted slightly toward blue (240° hue, 0.005 chroma). No secondary accent colors. Tier identifiers (Creator copper, Studio amber, Studio Pro gold) are reserved markers, not decoration.
- **Typography:** distinctive heading face paired with a refined neutral body. Mono for labels and codes. Avoid the obvious sans defaults.
- **Motion:** restrained. State changes only. No scroll-jacked reveals, no parallax, no decorative scanlines.

### Design Principles

1. **Set-and-leave reads visually.** Async-first surfaces, observational defaults, no nudges that demand the user check back. Calendars, queues, and analytics should look like a control room you walked into, not a feed you have to refresh.

2. **One accent, used sparingly.** The 60-30-10 rule is real. Blue is rare. When it appears, it means something — primary action, live state, "this is the thing."

3. **No fantasy aesthetic, but the dragon mark stays.** Mature execution of the brand. The mark is the only ornament; everything else earns its place.

4. **Don's hard rules.** No em-dashes in marketing copy. No fiction in feature lists. Every claim must be verifiable in shipped code.

5. **Mobile is the default viewing context.** Most prospects open the marketing site on a phone during a TikTok scroll break. Hero must land in one mobile viewport. Pricing must be readable without zoom.

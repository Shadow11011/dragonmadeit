A — Cinematic Void

Inspired by: Runway + Framer

Pure black canvas. Content floats in the void. Tight, compressed headlines with negative letter-spacing. The product screenshots ARE the design — everything else retreats. One accent color (fire orange), used surgically. Uppercase labels, dramatic scale contrast. Feels like a film studio, not a SaaS.

Best for: making DragonMadeIt feel premium and serious. The "this tool means business" vibe.

B — Warm Dark (Current + Refined)

Inspired by: Spotify + Warp

Keep the warm dark palette we already built, but refine it with Spotify's content-first philosophy and Warp's terminal-precision. Pill-shaped buttons, heavy shadows on elevated elements, warm-tinted neutrals. The dashboard mock becomes the hero art. Feels approachable but professional — "this is your automation command center."

Best for: balance of friendly + professional. The "easy to use but powerful" vibe. Least work since foundation exists.

C — Editorial Magazine

Inspired by: Vercel + Runway (editorial mode)

Asymmetric layouts. Serif display font mixed with sans-serif body. Newspaper-like information density with generous white space around it. The site reads like a well-designed magazine article about automation. Uppercase brand labels, dramatic size contrast between headings and body. Side-by-side content blocks instead of stacked sections.

Best for: standing out from every other SaaS. The "this doesn't look like any tool I've seen" vibe. Most distinctive but most work.
